### Phase 1a — the 3D view (runtime steps 1–2)

- **Two canvases, guarded.** `families/arcade3d.ts` puts the world canvas on the page at import
  (`game/world3dCanvas.ts`: fixed, full-window, `z-index: -1`, hit-testable) — before the Pixi
  context exists — so `pixi-svelte`'s auto-opaque check finds a non-ancestor under its canvas and
  creates the context **with alpha**. No `?opaque=0`, no shared file. While the 3D field is
  mounted the stage sets the coded `<Background>` band (`LAYER_BAND_BACKGROUND_CODED`) to
  `renderable = false`, so a layout needs no empty background scene.
- **The stage** (`ArcadeStage3D.svelte`): offline it runs `engine-3d` `createPlay3d` — one
  `motion3d` player on the placeholder arena (`sampleArena3d`: floor with a pit, walls, a 2.5 m tier
  with two ramps, a stair, a slab, pillars, a trampoline) at the fixed 60 Hz step, interpolated,
  catching up at most 250 ms; arrows / WASD move relative to the camera, Space jumps, Shift
  sprints (`listenKeys3d`; stick → int8 axes in `moveAxes`). Online it renders the stock
  `<ArcadeStage>` (the 3D room is runtime step 4). It registers the determinism probe
  (`state().arcade`: steps, the player's x / y / z).
- **The world** (`mountWorld3d({ canvas })`): `setLevel(colliders)` draws boxes and outward-wound
  ramp wedges; `sync(bodies, view)` moves one capsule-with-a-nose per body and places the camera;
  `pick(clientX, clientY)` ray-casts the level, else the ground plane; `setAim` shows a ring.
  1024² PCF shadows (the `half` column until T1 sets caps).
- **Camera rig:** `camera.js` follows the player in metres (dead zone, smoothing, look-ahead) and
  `rigPosition` places the camera from pitch / yaw / distance / FOV, read from the play-field
  screen's `camera.rig3d` block (`resolveRig3d`; every field optional, clamped).
- **Pointer forwarding:** a pointer on the Pixi canvas where `rootBoundary.hitTest` finds no
  interactive object (or only the stage) aims in the world; the player turns toward the point.
- **DPR, resize, quality, pause:** the 3D canvas renders at `min(device DPR, 1.5)` × the 2D
  render-quality controller's current/start resolution (floor ×0.5), while the HUD keeps its own;
  re-fitted on resize. A hidden tab steps and draws nothing and resumes where it stopped.
- **Gate:** `pnpm check:engine-3d` (50 checks; discovered by `check:all`): rig resolve and clamps,
  camera placement, camera-relative int8 axes, facing, the offline loop (lands, walks, jumps
  ~1.5 m, climbs the ramp to 2.5 m, respawns past `killY`, caps catch-up, interpolates), the follow.
  `scripts/playtest/arcade3d-play.mjs` boots a build and plays it (`--serve` to play by hand).

## Open items / next

1. **Tier chooser + caps (T1)** in `engine-3d`, reading `constants-shared/modelTiers.ts` (session
   1c's), with its fixture; then the DPR cap and shadow size come from the tier instead of the
   constants in `ArcadeStage3D.svelte` / `mountWorld3d`.
2. **Levels are a placeholder.** The play field shows `sampleArena3d()` until levels are data
   (gameplay §5: the seeded generator, then the Blender import). The arcade sample's 2D flow still
   spawns into its spawn layer (capped, never moved, off screen) — a 3D layout authors no 2D spawners.
3. **Online 3D** (runtime step 4, with session 1b's room): the stage's online branch is still the
   2D stock stage.
4. Desktop / delivery builds: `buildFamilies()` infers families from a baked config; the same
   inference must turn `PUBLIC_IE_3D` on once the config switch exists (the gameplay report names it).
5. Make `runtime-bytes` a required check (it always reports; ~4.5 min on CI when engine paths change,
   seconds otherwise) — an owner ruleset change. Until it is, the manifests drift: #1699 and #1709
   merged after #1707's manifests were recorded and `main`'s own run failed; #1712 re-recorded them
   from `main`'s artifact.
